Hello! I’m Zimo (Zina) Ma, a recent graduate from the University of Chicago, where I earned a Master of Arts in Computational Social Science in June 2025. My academic and professional journey blends data science with social impact, aiming to decode complex human behaviors and drive equitable solutions.

🎓 Education

I hold a Bachelor of Arts in Mathematics and Psychology from DePauw University, and recently completed my Master’s at UChicago, where I focused on applying computational methods to real-world social challenges. My training bridges quantitative modeling, behavioral science, and statistical programming.

🔬 Research Experience

At the University of Chicago, my MA thesis explored visual stereotypes in hiring, examining how facial features—like attractiveness and gender—affect perceived job suitability across client-facing and back-office roles. I used machine learning models and interactive visualizations to uncover bias patterns and propose fairer hiring strategies.

Previously, at DePauw, I served as a Research Analyst on two projects:
- Investigating gender disparities in natural disaster mortality among Indian women using GIS and statistical methods.
- Analyzing how COVID-19 impacted STEM education through survey-based statistical analysis (Chi-Square, t-tests).
